# Shipped — feature portfolio

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Goals & Outcomes** | You define company goals (e.g. "Weekly active teams to 1,000 by end of Q1"): name + metric + target + deadline. Tasks and projects get attached to a specific goal — so the team's work is tied to outcomes, not a loose list of features. In a single view you see the current metric value and everything the team is doing to move it. |
| **Post-Ship Impact Detector** | When tasks attached to a goal get marked as shipped, Shipped collects data for 14 days: who used the feature, how they behaved, how the goal metric changed for them vs. users who didn't use the feature. After 14 days you get a report in your inbox: "this change moved the metric by X% in the group that used it vs. control." |
| **Outcome Roadmap** | A roadmap view organized by goals, not by features. For each goal you see: how much work is planned under it, when specific things hit production, how much confidence the team has that this work will actually move the metric. |
| **Task-to-Event Linking** | When you spec a task, you pick a specific event from PostHog/Mixpanel/Amplitude that this task is supposed to move. For example, link the task "Add video to onboarding" to the event "user_completed_onboarding." Shipped later uses that link to know which metric to watch after this specific change ships. |
| **AI Daily Brief** | In the morning you get a message (email or Slack) summarizing: where outcomes are on track, where something went wrong, which tasks are worth prioritizing right now because the data suggests they move the most. Generated by AI from your data. |
| **MCP Server** | Shipped exposes an interface (MCP — Model Context Protocol, the standard AI assistants use to connect to tools) through which Claude, Cursor, and other AI assistants can read and write Shipped data without ever opening the Shipped UI. You type into Claude "what's happening with our weekly-active-teams goal?" and Claude pulls the answer from Shipped. It can also create tasks, read reports, and check goal status. |
| **Analytics Integrations** | Built-in connections to popular tools: PostHog, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, Segment. Shipped reads events and metrics from them, uses that for goal progress and the Impact Detector. It also goes the other way: a group of users affected by a Shipped task can land as a cohort (a group of users defined by a shared trait) in PostHog. |
| **Data Warehouse Integration** | Connect Shipped directly to your company's data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks). You point Shipped at the tables that hold events, users, business dimensions. Everything else (Goals, Impact Detector) works the same — just on your own data. |
| **Slack Integration** | Slack notifications when something happens with your goals: a post-ship report is ready, a metric dropped below a threshold, somebody attached a new task under a goal. You can route each goal to its own Slack channel so the right team sees the right alerts. |

## Table-stakes features

Required to compete with Linear / Asana / Jira, but not what makes Shipped different:

- Tasks, Projects, Cycles
- Comments, mentions
- In-app and email notifications
- Search
- Templates
- API + Webhooks
